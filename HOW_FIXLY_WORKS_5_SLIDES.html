Under the Hood: How Data Flows Without Breaking

How Fixly handles thousands of concurrent requests, live GPS updates, and financial transactions with sub-second response times and zero data loss.

Architecture Flow
📱 1. The Clients

Flutter Mobile App: iOS & Android apps for customers & workers.
React Dashboard: Browser portal for federation managers.

➔
⚡ 2. Real-Time Engine

Node.js + WebSockets: Live two-way highway. Coordinates matching, live GPS broadcasting, and auth verification.

➔
⚡ Redis Cache

Sub-second live GPS, active voice AI session state, rapid job lock timers.

🗄️ MongoDB Vault

Permanent truth: verified KYC, payment ledgers, bookings & audit trail.

🔄

Zero-Loss Job Queues

Background worker queues handle SMS alerts, push notifications (FCM), and invoice receipts asynchronously. Even if a worker passes through a network dead zone, events retry safely until acknowledged.
🛡️ Resilient against spotty mobile connectivity
🔒

Tamper-Proof Ledger

Every fee deduction, tip, and wallet balance credit executes with strict database consistency. Money cannot be credited twice or lost, and payouts reflect instantly upon OTP customer confirmation.
⚖️ Audited balance for every single rupee
📞

Private WebRTC Calling

Calls between customers and workers run peer-to-peer over encrypted WebRTC voice channels. Neither party ever sees the other's real phone number, preventing harassment and spam.
🔐 100% privacy & zero telecom number leak
